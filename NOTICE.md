# Source acknowledgments

Research synthesis began with a request to turn a collection of research tasks into a reusable skill. The repository contains rewritten workflow instructions and fictional evaluation material. It does not distribute the original prompt collection, third-party skill implementations, papers, or assessment checklists.

The workflow topics include common research operations: screening, extraction, comparing measures and methods, appraisal, synthesis, question development, and source checking. The original prompt collection's authorship was not established; this project does not claim ownership of it or license it.

The [methodology notes](skills/research-synthesis/references/methodology-sources.md) acknowledge the external guidance used in designing the workflows, including Cochrane, PRISMA, JBI, Trent University, the Agent Skills specification, and Anthropic's skill-engineering article. Linked source materials remain subject to their respective terms. The repository's MIT license does not grant rights to those materials, research papers supplied by users, or external software named in installation examples.

All authors, papers, and results in the evaluation cases are fictional. They are test data, not citations or research evidence. No affiliation with or endorsement by the named organizations is implied.
