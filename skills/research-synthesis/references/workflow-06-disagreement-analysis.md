# Workflow 06 - Disagreement analysis

Diagnose apparent disagreement without vote counting or significance-based conclusions.

## Parameters and delivery

- Required: finding A versus finding B, or enough source detail to identify both precisely.
- Follow the main delivery mode and existing project convention; save only when authorized,
  optionally to `research/<corpus-key>/<question-key>/notes/disagreements.md`.
  No prior comparison table, manifest, or pipeline is required.

## Procedure

1. Use stable IDs and verified extracts; targeted rereading is allowed to verify results,
   uncertainty, methods, locators, and source versions. State access limitations.
2. Restate each side accurately: construct, population, setting, comparator, timepoint,
   estimand, direction, magnitude, uncertainty, adjustment, and design where applicable.
   For qualitative/theoretical disagreement, state the actual interpretations/propositions
   and contextual support instead of imposing numerical fields.
3. Compare definitions, sampling, context, timing, design, measurement, analyses,
   and source/sample dependence. Reuse an applicable definitions table if present.
4. Assign one or more contributing classifications with source-based reasons:
   - **Direct contradiction:** incompatible claims under sufficiently comparable scope,
     estimand, and evidence; opposing point estimates alone do not establish this.
   - **Scope difference:** different populations, settings, conditions, or time windows.
   - **Measurement mismatch:** different operationalizations or construct meanings.
   - **Design/analysis difference:** different estimands, controls, models, or implementation.
   - **Unresolved:** uncertainty or missing evidence prevents adjudication; name what is missing.
   Allow interacting causes and an unresolved residual; do not force a single category.
5. Never infer conflict or absence of effect from significant versus nonsignificant
   results. Compare estimates and interval uncertainty; a subgroup contrast needs an
   appropriate interaction/difference analysis. Do not use observed-effect post-hoc power
   to diagnose a null. Discuss precision, design, and reported prospective planning instead.
6. Rank plausible explanations by their actual support, distinguishing documented
   differences from hypotheses about why those differences matter. Do not turn a
   speculative mechanism or measure-insensitivity explanation into a demonstrated cause.
7. State what additional evidence would distinguish explanations, whether the supplied
   corpus contains it, and what remains unresolved. Do not manufacture a consensus.

## Output structure

- Precise statements of A and B, with source locators and uncertainty/access limits.
- Comparison table: `aspect, source_A, source_B, comparability_consequence, locators`.
- Contributing classifications, their support, and unresolved residual uncertainty.
- Ranked explanations, evidence for/against each, and discriminating evidence needed.

## Completion checks

- Both sides are faithfully represented and source-traceable.
- Multiple causes can coexist; demonstrated differences are separate from explanations.
- No significance vote, unsupported null-as-no-effect claim, or post-hoc power diagnosis.
- Dependence, comparable estimands, and missing adjudication evidence are considered.
